- [ ] **GameChanger stats: decide where they live and how they're used.** The "GameChanger stats" button on the player card was removed for now. The stats popup (`#stats-modal`, `openStatsModal` in `static/app.js`) and the Admin → Stats CSV import still work, but there is no way to open the popup from the player page. Decide where stats should appear (for example a Stats tab on Player Development, a Reports page, or game results on the Schedule) and how coaches enter or view them, then reconnect it.
- [ ] **Import calendars from Google, Outlook, Yahoo, etc.** Let coaches bring outside calendars into the Schedule (for example by pasting an iCal/ICS subscription link or connecting an account) so games and practices don't have to be typed in by hand. Decide whether it's a one-time import or a synced subscription, and which team the imported events belong to.
- [ ] **Create a repository of practices.** A library of saved practice plans (drills, order, timing, equipment, notes) that coaches can browse, reuse, and attach to practices on the Schedule. Decide how it relates to the Drills page and whether plans are shared across teams.
- [ ] **Import a player's photo.** Let coaches upload a photo for each player so it shows on her player card, the roster, and the admin player table instead of just initials. Decide who can upload (coach only, or the player herself), size/format limits, and where photos are stored.
- [ ] **Create a coaches chatroom.** A private place in the app where coaches and staff can message each other about lineups, practices, and players. Decide whether it's one room per team or one for all staff, whether it needs notifications, and how long messages are kept.
- [ ] **Make social media posts.** Help coaches create shareable posts (game results, player shout-outs, upcoming events) as images or text ready for Instagram, Facebook, X, etc. Decide whether the app posts directly or just produces something to download and share, and how player privacy/consent is handled.
- [ ] **Add parents' contact information.** Store parent/guardian names, phone numbers, and emails for each player so coaches can reach families quickly. Decide how many contacts per player, who can see them (coaches only vs. all staff), and whether parents get their own logins or notifications.
- [ ] **Contacts list.** One searchable directory of everyone the team deals with: players, parents, coaches and staff, plus outside contacts like league officials, umpires, field managers, and opposing coaches. Decide how it ties in with the parents' contact information above, who can see which contacts, and whether it can export to a phone's contacts.
- [ ] **Create a Resources tab.** A sidebar page for useful team material: rule books, league documents, forms and waivers, training videos, and helpful links. Decide whether it's shared across all teams or per team, who can add or remove items, and whether players and parents can see it.
- [ ] **Retail and merch.** A team store for jerseys, hats, spirit wear, and fundraiser items that families can browse and order from. Decide whether it links out to an existing store (e.g. a team shop site) or takes orders in the app, how sizes and player names/numbers are collected, and how payments and order deadlines are handled.
